# Preface

At the end of 2022, the emergence of ChatGPT changed people's understanding of artificial intelligence and also brought phased changes to the field of natural language processing (NLP). Large Language Model (LLM) represented by the GPT series model has become the mainstream research in NLP and even the field of artificial intelligence. Since 2023, LLM has always been the core topic in the field of artificial intelligence, triggering round after round of technology waves.

LLM is actually a derivative result of the pretrain Language Model (PLM) of classic research methods in the field of NLP. The NLP field focuses on the processing, understanding and generation of natural language texts written by humans. From its birth to this day, it has undergone many changes in the symbolism stage, statistical learning stage, deep learning stage, pre-training model stage to the current big model stage. PLM represented by GPT and BERT is the core research result of the NLP field in the previous stage. It uses the attention mechanism as the model architecture and uses the pre-training-fine-tuning stage idea to achieve strong natural language understanding ability through self-supervised pre-training on a large number of unsupervised texts. However, traditional PLM still relies on a certain amount of supervised data for fine-tuning downstream tasks, and its performance in natural language generation tasks is not satisfactory. The performance of NLP systems is still far from the general artificial intelligence that people expect.

LLM is a breakthrough achievement based on PLM by expanding the scale of model parameters, pre-training data, and introducing fine-tuning instructions, human feedback reinforcement learning and other means. Compared with traditional PLM, LLM has emergence capabilities, strong context learning capabilities, instruction comprehension capabilities and text generation capabilities. In the big model stage, NLP researchers can abandon a large amount of supervised data annotation work to a certain extent. By providing a small number of supervised examples, LLM can achieve performance comparable to large-scale fine-tuning PLM on designated downstream tasks. At the same time, powerful instruction comprehension and text generation capabilities enable LLM to respond directly, efficiently and accurately to the goal of general artificial intelligence.

LLM's breakthrough progress has stimulated research popularity in the NLP field and even the entire field of artificial intelligence. Universities, research institutes, large factories and many traditional fields have been invested in the wave of LLM research. Since 2023, LLM has been emerging in stages, and model performance has been constantly refreshing its upper limit. From ChatGPT at the beginning, to GPT-4, to more powerful and customized models represented by DeepSeek-R1, multimodal large models represented by Qwen-VL, and other more powerful and customized models, LLM applications have also continuously emerged innovative applications that can enhance actual productivity and empower users' actual lives. From the "Hundred Models War" to the "First Year of Agent", LLM base research may have become a stable pattern, and LLM research has always been in full swing. What is certain is that in the not distant future, LLM and LLM-based applications will definitely become the infrastructure in people's lives and are inseparable from everyone's life, study, and work.

In this context, the ability to deeply understand and master the principles of LLM and be able to manually apply and train any LLM is crucial to every NLP researcher and even AI researchers in other fields. At the end of 2023, we created two original open source model tutorials: self-llm (open source model edible guide: https://github.com/datawhalechina/self-llm) and llm-universe (hands-on learning model application development: https://github.com/datawhalechina/llm-universe). The former aims to provide developers with one-stop open source LLM deployment, reasoning, and fine-tuning tutorials, and the latter aims to guide developers to build their own LLM applications from scratch. Both tutorials have helped a wide range of domestic and foreign developers, and have also received support and recognition from many developers. In the feedback from learners, we found that there is currently a lack of a complete tutorial to explain the principles of LLM from scratch and guide learners to build and train LLM by themselves.

In view of this, we have written this tutorial combining the principles of LLM and practical practice. This book will start from the basic research methods of NLP, and go deeper layer by layer based on the ideas and principles of LLM, and analyze the architectural foundation and training process of LLM for readers in turn. At the same time, we will combine the most mainstream code framework in the LLM field to practice how to build and train an LLM by hand, in order to achieve the goal of teaching it to fish and also teach it to fish. I hope readers can start from this book to enter the vast world of LLM and explore the endless possibilities of LLM.

## Suggestions for readers

This book contains the theoretical basis, principles introduction and project practice of LLM. The book includes the analysis of core ideas, formula analysis and code practice of LLM and NLP. It aims to help developers deeply understand and master the basic principles and applications of LLM. Therefore, this book is suitable for college students, researchers, and LLM enthusiasts. Before reading this book, you need to have a certain degree of programming experience, especially to have a certain understanding of the Python programming language. At the same time, you'd better have the knowledge of deep learning and understand the concepts and terms related to the NLP field to make it easier to read the book.

This book is divided into two parts - basic knowledge and practical application. Chapter 1 to 4 are the basic knowledge part, introducing the basic principles of LLM from a shallow to deeper perspective. Among them, Chapter 1 briefly introduces the basic tasks and development of NLP, providing reference for researchers in non-NLP fields; Chapter 2 introduces the basic architecture of LLM - Transformer, including principle introduction and code implementation, as the most important theoretical basis of LLM; Chapter 3 introduces the classic PLM in an overall manner, including three architectures: Encoder-Only, Encoder-Decoder and Decoder-Only, and also introduces the architecture and ideas of some current mainstream LLM; Chapter 4 officially enters the LLM part, introducing in detail the characteristics, capabilities and overall training process of LLM. Chapter 5~Chapter 7 is the practical application part, which will gradually lead readers to the underlying details of LLM. Among them, Chapter 5 will lead readers to build an LLM based on the PyTorch layer and realize the entire process of pre-training and supervised fine-tuning; Chapter 6 will introduce the industry's mainstream LLM training framework Transformers, leading readers to quickly and efficiently implement the LLM training process based on the framework; Chapter 7 will introduce various applications based on LLM to complete readers' understanding of the LLM system, including LLM evaluation, retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and agents and simple implementation. You can selectively read relevant chapters based on your personal interests and needs.

During the process of reading this book, it is recommended that you combine theory with practice. LLM is a rapidly developing and practical field. We recommend that you invest more in practice, reproduce the various codes provided in this book, and actively participate in LLM-related projects and competitions, and truly invest in the wave of LLM development. We encourage you to follow Datawhale and other LLM-related open source communities. When you encounter problems, you can ask questions in the Datawhale community at any time. Datawhale will always follow the development of LLM and other artificial intelligence technologies. You are welcome to follow or join the co-construction of the Datawhale community.

Finally, every reader is welcome to join the ranks of LLM developers after reading this book. As a domestic AI open source community, we hope to gather co-creators to enrich this world of open source LLM and create more and more comprehensive tutorials on LLM. Sparks dotted, converging into the sea. We hope to be the ladder for LLM and the general public, embrace the more magnificent and vast LLM world with an open source spirit of freedom and equality.
